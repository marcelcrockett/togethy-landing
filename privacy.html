Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 24, 2026

At Togethy, we take your family's privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the Togethy app and website.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Account information — email address and password
  • Household information — household name and member names
  • App content — calendar events, tasks, bills, expenses, and documents you create
  • Financial connection data — when you connect a bank account via Plaid, we store an access token (not your banking credentials)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Togethy service
  • Sync your household data across devices and family members
  • Send push notifications and reminders you've opted into
  • Respond to your support requests
  • Process subscription payments through Stripe

3. Banking & Financial Data

Togethy uses Plaid to enable bank account connections. When you connect a financial institution:

  • Your banking credentials are never stored by Togethy
  • Plaid handles all authentication directly with your bank
  • We store only an encrypted access token to retrieve your balance and transaction data
  • You can disconnect any bank account at any time from within the app

Plaid's privacy policy is available at plaid.com/legal.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, which uses industry-standard encryption at rest and in transit. Documents uploaded to the Document Vault are stored in encrypted cloud storage. We use row-level security to ensure each household can only access their own data.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your data with advertisers. We share data only with:

  • Supabase — database and storage provider
  • Plaid — financial data connection (when you choose to connect a bank)
  • Stripe — payment processing
  • Apple/Google — app distribution platforms

6. Children's Privacy

Togethy is designed for household management by adults. Children's profiles within a household are created and managed by adult household administrators. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about your household
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Export your household data
  • Disconnect any connected bank accounts

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@togethy.app.

8. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, we permanently delete all associated household data within 30 days.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via email or in-app notification. Continued use of Togethy after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at hello@togethy.app
